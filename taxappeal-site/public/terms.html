Terms

The short version of what we do, how we are paid, and what nobody can promise. The agreement you sign before we file is the contract.

Last updated 7 Nov 2026

What we do

Check your assessment against county records and, if the case holds and you sign, appeal it for you.

How we are paid

A share of the first year's savings, only if your value goes down. The share is written in your agreement.

If nothing changes

You owe nothing. Not the filing fee, not our time.

Who we are

Claim OS is a private company. We are not affiliated with any government agency, county property appraiser or value adjustment board, and nothing we send you is an official notice. We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. A licensed tax professional is available on request before you sign.

By using this site you agree to these terms. The appeal itself is governed by the agreement you sign before we file; where the two differ, the agreement wins.

What we do

We compare your assessment with the county's own roll and with recent sales of similar homes, and tell you whether your case looks strong, possible, or not worth filing. Weak cases are dropped before filing, and we say so.

If the case holds and you sign our agreement and, where the county requires it, a DR-486POA form, we assemble the evidence, file the petition with the county's value adjustment board before its deadline, pay the filing fee, and represent the case at the hearing. You hear from us by email at every step.

How we are paid

Nothing upfront. If the county lowers your value, we invoice a share of the first year's tax savings once your lower bill is real. The share is written in the agreement you sign before we file; nothing on this site changes it. If the value stays where it is, you owe nothing.

No guarantee

Only the county decides your value. The figures on this site are estimates from public records and recent sales; they can be out of date or wrong, and an estimate is not a promise. The board rules on the evidence, and a value can in principle move either way, which is why we drop weak cases before filing.

Your choices

  • Withdraw. You can withdraw the petition any time before the hearing by emailing us. We withdraw it, and nothing is owed.
  • Ask before you sign. Request a call with a licensed tax professional; we arrange it by email.
  • Delete. Ask us to remove what you typed; the Privacy page says how.

Governing law

These terms, and the agreement, are governed by the laws of the State of Florida.
