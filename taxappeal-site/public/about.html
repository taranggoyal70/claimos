A small team, mostly software.

Claim OS was built at the Naïve Autonomous Company Hackathon at Y Combinator in November 2026. Agents do the scanning, drafting and follow-up; people approve every email that goes out and every filing.

Who does what

The software does the volume. A person signs off on anything that reaches you or the county.

Agents do
  • Scan public county rolls for homes valued above similar recent sales
  • Compare each case with neighbors' assessments and drop the weak ones
  • Draft the emails you receive and the evidence packet we file
  • Chase the county for hearing dates and decisions
People do
  • Read and approve every email before it leaves our inbox
  • Confirm your parcel against the county roll by hand
  • Sign the petition and any form the county asks for
  • Answer you when the question is hard

Nothing is sent and nothing is filed on your home without a person looking at it first.

Rules we hold ourselves to

Written down so you can hold us to them.

A private company

Claim OS is not affiliated with any government agency, county property appraiser or value adjustment board, and it is not a law firm.

No fee outside the agreement you sign

We are paid a share of the first year's savings, only if your value goes down. The share is in the agreement, nowhere else.

No promised outcomes

Every figure on this site is an estimate from public records. The county decides your value, and we say so every time we show a number.

No cold calls or texts

We write by email. We call only when you wrote your number down and asked us to.

Public records plus what you typed, nothing more

We hold the county's roll data on your parcel and what you entered on this site. We never buy, sell or share it.

The county's dates, never ours

The only deadlines we mention are the county's own. There is no countdown and no offer that expires.

Write to us.

We reply by email from hello@claim-os-d4myby.usenaive.ai, usually within a day or two, and we only call when you asked us to.
