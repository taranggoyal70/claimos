What the share covers

Everything it takes to put a case in front of the county. Nothing you did not sign for.

Included

  • The parcel check against the county rollDone by hand before we ask you to sign anything.
  • The evidence packetComparable sales, assessment ratios, and anything you send us, in one file.
  • The petition and its filing feeFiled with the county's value adjustment board before its deadline. We pay the fee.
  • Representation at the hearingA special magistrate hears both sides. We present the evidence.
  • An email at every stepFrom claimos@agentmail.to. No calls unless you asked for one.
  • A licensed tax professional, on requestTick the box on the form and one will contact you.

Not included

  • Legal adviceClaim OS is not a law firm. If your case needs a lawyer, we say so.
  • Your tax bill itselfThe county bills you. We work on the value behind the bill, not the bill.
  • Tax years already closedOnce the county's petition window has closed, we cannot file for that year.
  • Anything you did not sign forThe agreement lists the work and the share. Nothing is added later.

Who files, and in whose name

Claim OS files as your agent, with a DR-486POA where the county requires one. The petition is in your name, and you can withdraw it before the hearing. If a case looks weak once we have checked the parcel, we drop it before filing and tell you why. Dropped or withdrawn, you owe nothing.

Why there is no number on this page

The share depends on the county and the tax year, so it lives in the agreement, where you can read it before you sign anything. We invoice it once, after the county's decision is final and your lower bill is real. If the value does not move, there is no invoice.

Questions about money

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. If your value goes down we keep a share of the first year's savings; the exact terms are in the agreement you sign before we file.

When do I pay?

After the county's decision is final and your lower bill is real. We invoice our share then, once. If the value does not move, there is no invoice.

Can my value go up because I appealed?

The board rules on the evidence; a value can in principle move either way, which is why we drop weak cases before filing and tell you when we do.
