Four things happen. You do one of them.

1

We find

We scan public county rolls for homes valued above comparable sales.

2

We check

We compare your value with recent sales of similar homes nearby. If the case looks weak, we say so.

3

We file

We prepare the evidence and file the petition with the county before its own deadline. You sign; we do the paperwork.

4

You save

If the county lowers your value, your bill goes down. We keep a share of the first year's savings; terms in the agreement.

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. If your value goes down we keep a share of the first year's savings; the exact terms are in the agreement you sign before we file.

How long does it take?

The county sets the dates: petitions are heard in the months after filing closes. We email you at every step.

What do you need from me?

Your address, an email, and a signed agreement. Everything else comes from public records.
