Questions owners ask us.

Plain answers about money, timing, paperwork and who we are. If yours is missing, email us and we will answer it, then add it here.

Money

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront, and nothing at all if the county keeps your value where it is. If the value goes down, we keep a share of the first year's savings; the exact share is written in the agreement you sign before anything is filed. For scale: among the strong cases our scan has identified so far (none filed yet), the median estimated saving is about $1,037 a year. Your own figure depends on your parcel, and the county decides.

When do I pay?

Only after the county has lowered your value and your tax bill shows it. We invoice our share of that first year's saving then; from the second year on, every dollar is yours. If nothing changes, you owe nothing and there is nothing to cancel.

Can my value go up because I appealed?

The value adjustment board rules on the evidence, so in principle a value can move either way. That is exactly why we drop weak cases before filing and only go forward where recent sales and neighbouring assessments support a lower number. You can also withdraw the petition at any point before the hearing.

Timing

How long does it take?

The county sets the calendar, not us. TRIM notices arrive in August, the petition deadline is about 25 days later, hearings run through the fall and winter, and the lower value reaches your bill once the board's decision is final. We email you at every step, and you can follow progress on Track my claim.

I missed the petition deadline. Now what?

For this tax year the window is most likely closed: a late petition is accepted only for a good cause the board agrees with, and we do not file petitions the board will dismiss. Leave your address anyway. We keep the case open and check the roll again when next year's notices come out, and you decide then whether to go ahead.

What we need

What do you need from me?

Your address and an email to start. If the case holds, one signed agreement and, where your county asks for it, a DR-486POA form. Everything else, the sales, the assessments and the petition itself, comes from public records and our work. We may email you a question or two along the way.

What is a DR-486POA?

The Florida Department of Revenue's power-of-attorney form for value adjustment board petitions. It authorises Claim OS to file and speak for you on this petition, and nothing else. Some counties require it from any representative; when yours does, we send it pre-filled together with the agreement, and you sign both.

I have a homestead cap. Does this still help?

The Save Our Homes cap limits how fast your assessed value can rise each year, so it already protects many long-time owners. If the cap holds your taxable value below the value we could argue for, there may be no saving this year, and the check says so: that is what the "Likely no savings this year" result means. We do not file in that case.

Your home

I own a condo. Does this work?

Yes. Condos are often where the clearest cases are, because many near-identical units sell in the same building and the comparison is strong. When several units share one street address, the check asks which one is yours before it shows a number.

I live out of state.

That is fine. The whole process runs by email, and you do not need to attend the hearing; we represent you with the signed agreement and, where the county asks for it, the DR-486POA. Your mailing address on the roll stays as it is. All we need is an email address you actually read.

How did you find my home?

From the county property appraiser's public roll and recorded sales, the same records anyone can look up. We compare each home's assessed value with recent nearby sales and with similar homes' assessments, and flag the ones that stand out. We do not buy lists and we never call or text owners; if you heard from us, it was by mail or email, or because you checked your address here.

Us

Are you the county?

No. Claim OS is a private company and is not affiliated with any government agency, county property appraiser or value adjustment board. We never collect your tax, and nothing we send you is a notice from the county. Your county's own notices keep arriving exactly as before.

Are you lawyers?

No. Claim OS is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. We prepare the evidence and file the petition as your representative. A licensed tax professional is available on request, and for a legal question you should speak to an attorney.

What do you store about me?

What you typed on this site, the public roll data for your parcel, and the emails we exchange. We never sell or share it, and we do not contact you about anything other than your claim. Email claimos@agentmail.to and we delete it. The short version of our privacy page is the long version.
