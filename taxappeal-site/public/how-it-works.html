Eight steps. Most of them are ours.

The whole loop, from the county roll to a lower bill: what we do at each step, and the little we need from you. Four of the eight need nothing from you at all.

Who does what, step by step

  1. Find
  2. Qualify
  3. Sign up
  4. Evidence
  5. File
  6. Waiting on the county
  7. Appeal again
  8. Won and paid
  1. 1

    Find

    Claim OS

    We scan the county's public roll for homes valued above what similar homes nearby have recently sold for.

    You

    Nothing.

  2. 2

    Qualify

    Claim OS

    We compare your assessment with your neighbors' and with recent sales. Weak cases are dropped before anyone is contacted, and we say so.

    You

    Nothing.

  3. 3

    Sign up

    Claim OS

    You check your address and leave an email. We confirm the parcel by hand against the county roll and send the agreement to sign. Nothing is filed before you sign.

    You

    Ten seconds on the site, then one signed agreement and, where the county asks for it, a DR-486POA form that lets us file on your behalf.

  4. 4

    Evidence

    Claim OS

    We assemble comparable sales, assessment ratios and anything you send us into one packet the board can follow.

    You

    Answer an email or two if we have a question about the home.

  5. 5

    File

    Claim OS

    We file the petition with the county's value adjustment board before its deadline and pay the filing fee.

    You

    Nothing.

  6. 6

    Waiting on the county

    Claim OS

    The board sets a hearing and a special magistrate hears both sides. We present the packet and follow up with the county until it answers.

    You

    Nothing. We email you the hearing date and the decision.

  7. 7

    Appeal again

    Claim OS

    If the first decision is weak, we judge whether a second round is worth it on the evidence, and tell you plainly either way.

    You

    Decide with us.

  8. 8

    Won and paid

    Claim OS

    Your value goes down and your bill follows. We invoice a share of the first year's savings; the terms are in the agreement you signed. If nothing changes, you owe nothing.

    You

    Pay once the lower bill is real.

The Florida calendar

The county's dates decide the pace, not ours. Here is the year as it runs in most Florida counties.

August TRIM notices

The county mails its Notice of Proposed Property Taxes. Your new value is on it, and the clock starts.

About 25 days later Petition deadline

Petitions to the value adjustment board close roughly 25 days after the notices go out. We file before this date.

Fall to winter Hearings

A special magistrate hears the evidence from both sides, usually between October and the new year.

November Tax bills

Bills arrive in November. If your hearing is still ahead, the bill comes first and any reduction follows the decision.

Each county sets its own dates. We work to the county's, never ours.
